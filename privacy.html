Caravan Jam: Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. Applies to the Caravan Jam test builds (web, Android and iOS/TestFlight).

In short: Caravan Jam does not ask for your name, email or location, shows no ads, and does not track you across apps or websites. To learn whether the game is fun and fair, it sends anonymous gameplay events (such as "level 12 started" or "level 12 won with 3 stars") linked to a random ID the game creates on your device.

Who we are

Caravan Jam is a prototype puzzle game made by Metisian Works. Questions or deletion requests: metisianworks@gmail.com.

What the game collects

DataWhy
A random install ID and session ID, made on your device. They are not tied to your name, account, advertising ID or device hardware ID.To count players and sessions, and to tell whether people come back the next day.
Gameplay events: app opened, session started and ended (with its length), level started, won, failed, retried or left, tutorial steps, turntable taps, and the level number, stars, moves and time taken.To find levels that are too hard, too easy or confusing.
The platform (web, Android or iOS), the game version, your test group (for example "cj_first"), the time on your device and its time-zone offset.To compare versions and test groups, and to work out "the next day".

That is all. The game does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, advertising identifiers, or anything you type.

Where it goes

Events are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to a database run for us by Supabase, hosted in the United States. Like any web service, Supabase handles your IP address briefly to deliver the data. We don't store IP addresses with the events and don't use them to identify you. If you are offline, events wait on your device and are sent later.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad networks, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We never sell or share the data. It is used only to improve this game.
  • No account or sign-in.

Your progress

Your stars, settings and the random install ID are saved on your device only. Deleting the app (or, on the web, clearing this site's data) deletes them.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Gameplay events are kept only for the playtest and deleted within 12 months of being collected. Because they are anonymous, we can't look up "your" data by name. To have your events deleted sooner, write to the address above with roughly when and on which device you played, and we'll remove everything we can match.

Children

The test builds are not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will also be noted in the game's release notes.
